Privacy Policy

All About You Astrology, by Beyond Animations
Last updated: 13 November 2026

All About You Astrology does not collect your personal information. There is no account to create, no server to send anything to, and no analytics or advertising in the app. What you enter stays on your phone.

This policy explains that in full, including the one case where data can leave your device, so that nothing here is a surprise.

1. What the app stores

To calculate a birth chart, the app needs the information you type in:

  • A name or label you choose for each chart
  • A birth date
  • A birth time, if you know it
  • A birth place
  • Any folders you file charts into, and any notes or answers you give inside a reading
  • Your display settings, such as light or dark background

All of it is saved in the app's own private storage on your device, in an area that other apps cannot read. None of it is transmitted anywhere by the app.

2. What the app does not do

  • No accounts. You never create one, and there is nothing to sign in to.
  • No servers. Every chart, score and reading is calculated on your phone. The developer never receives your birth data and has no way to see it.
  • No analytics or tracking. There is no usage tracking, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier and no third-party software development kits of any kind in the app.
  • No advertising. The app shows no ads and sells no data. There is no data to sell.
  • No internet permission. As of this version the app declares no Android permissions at all, including internet access. It is technically unable to send your information anywhere.

3. The one way data can leave your device

Android Auto Backup

Android itself offers a backup feature so that your apps' data survives when you set up a new phone. If it is switched on for your device and for this app, Android — not the app — copies the app's saved data, which includes the birth information you have entered, to your own Google Drive account.

This is a feature of the Android operating system. The data goes to your own Google account, not to the developer, and the developer cannot access it. It is governed by Google's privacy policy rather than this one.

You can turn it off at any time. On most phones this is under Settings → Google → Backup, or Settings → System → Backup. Turning it off means your charts will not transfer automatically to a new phone, so you may wish to export them first — see below.

Sharing and exporting, when you choose it

The app can create a picture of a reading, or a file containing your saved charts, and hand it to another app through Android's share sheet. This only ever happens when you tap share or export, and you choose which app receives it. Once it leaves the app, what happens to it is governed by the app you sent it to and by your own choices.

4. Notifications

If you turn on the daily notification, your score for the day is calculated on your phone and shown by your phone. Nothing is sent to a server, and no push messaging service is involved. You can turn notifications off inside the app, or in your Android settings, at any time.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

If the app offers a paid subscription or purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment and tells the app only whether the purchase is active. The developer never receives or stores your card number, billing address or any other payment detail. Google's handling of that information is covered by Google's own privacy policy and the Google Play terms of service.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects nothing and transmits nothing, there is no children's data held anywhere to request the deletion of.

7. Your rights over your information

Because your information is held only on your own device, you are in direct control of it:

  • To see it — open the app.
  • To change it — edit the chart in the app.
  • To delete some of it — delete the chart in the app.
  • To delete all of it — uninstall the app, or clear its storage under Settings → Apps. This permanently removes everything the app has saved.
  • To take a copy with you — use the export option in the saved charts screen.

There is no request to send and no form to fill in, because there is no copy of your data anywhere else. If Android Auto Backup is on, a copy may also exist in your own Google Drive; you can remove that through your Google account settings.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
