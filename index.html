All About You Astrology

An Android app by Beyond Animations

All About You Astrology calculates your birth chart on your own phone and reads it back to you in plain language. Your daily vibe, a deep look at who you are, what is ahead, and who you vibe with.

Everything is worked out on the device. There is no account, no server, and the app collects no information about you.
